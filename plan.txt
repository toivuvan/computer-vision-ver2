Ke hoach hien tai: baseline YOLO-like ConvNeXt + centerness branch

1. Backbone
- ConvNeXt-Tiny pretrained ImageNet.
- Output C3/C4/C5 tu stride 8/16/32.

2. Neck
- YOLO-like PAN-FPN.
- Output P3/P4/P5.
- Co SPPF va C2PSA-lite.

3. Detection head
- Decoupled head theo tung scale.
- Classification branch: 5 class logits.
- Regression branch: l/t/r/b distance, positive bang exp(raw) * stride.
- Centerness branch: 1 quality logit, dung reg feature.

4. Assignment
- FCOS-style center sampling.
- Location phai:
  - nam trong bbox
  - nam trong scale range phu hop
  - nam trong center region radius = 1.5 * stride
- Neu mot location match nhieu bbox, chon bbox co dien tich nho nhat.
- Centerness target = sqrt(min(l,r)/max(l,r) * min(t,b)/max(t,b)).

5. Loss
- Focal loss cho classification.
- CIoU loss cho bbox regression.
- BCEWithLogits loss cho centerness, chi tinh tren positive locations.
- Tong loss = (cls + reg + centerness) / num_pos.

6. Inference
- score = sigmoid(class_logit) * sigmoid(centerness_logit).
- Decode l/t/r/b quanh grid center.
- Letterbox inverse ve kich thuoc anh goc.
- Class-wise Soft-NMS.
- Giu toi da 100 predictions moi anh.

7. Ly do them centerness
- Baseline 0.8035 co recall cao nhung precision thap.
- Centerness giup down-weight box co localization kem.
- Muc tieu: giam false positive ma khong lam mat recall qua nhieu.

8. Luu y train
- Phai train lai vao checkpoint folder moi, vi checkpoint cu thieu weights cua centerness branch.
- Lenh goi y:

python train.py --train_data ./public/annotations/train.json --val_data ./public/annotations/val.json --image_dir ./public/train/images --val_image_dir ./public/val/images --checkpoint_dir ./models_centerness/ --epochs 35 --batch_size 16 --img_size 416 --early_stop_patience 10 --min_epochs 25
